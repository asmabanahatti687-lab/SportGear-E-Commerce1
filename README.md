# ⚡ SportGear – Premier Sports & Fitness Equipment Frontend

SportGear is a fully responsive, feature-rich modern web frontend for a sports equipment, performance footwear, and activewear e-commerce platform. Built with **Tailwind CSS**, **Font Awesome**, and vanilla **JavaScript**, it provides a complete simulated e-commerce and administration experience directly in the browser.

## ✨ Key Features & Functionality

*   **1. Comprehensive Sports Catalog & Marketplace:**
    *   Browse certified athletic equipment and performance gear across 8+ distinct categories (Gym Equipment, Fitness Accessories, Sports Shoes, Sportswear, Cricket Equipment, Football Equipment, Badminton Equipment, and Yoga & Exercise).
    *   Dynamic real-time search bar with instant query filtering.
    *   Interactive max-price slider, stock status check, minimum star rating filters, and multi-parameter sorting (Price: Low to High, High to Low, Highest Rated, Featured).
*   **2. Interactive Shopping Cart & Checkout System:**
    *   Slide-out cart drawer featuring real-time subtotal, 5% estimated tax, and dynamic delivery fee calculation.
    *   Free shipping progress indicator bar unlocking free delivery for orders over ₹1,999.
    *   Working promo voucher support (`SPORT20` for 20% OFF).
    *   Simulated multi-step checkout with delivery address validation and payment modes (UPI / QR, Credit/Debit Card, Pay on Delivery).
    *   Instant order confirmation modal with printable invoice/receipt generation.
*   **3. Product Quick View & Saved Wishlist:**
    *   Full-screen product quick view modal displaying key specs, stock availability, and full descriptions.
    *   Interactive wishlist saving system with persistent badge counter.
*   **4. Store Administration Console (Admin Panel):**
    *   Dedicated administrative modal featuring store metrics (Total Catalog Items, Low Stock Alerts, and Gross Revenue).
    *   Full CRUD functionality: Add new products, update pricing/stock levels, and delete inventory items in real-time.
*   **5. User Authentication & Profile Dashboard:**
    *   Simulated user login and registration forms supporting Customer and Admin roles.
    *   Customer profile modal tracking past order histories and order status logs.
*   **6. Customer Reviews & Support:**
    *   Verified customer feedback highlights and interactive support ticket submission modal.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

*   **HTML5 & CSS3** (Semantic layout structure and custom responsive styling)
*   **Tailwind CSS** (v3 via CDN with custom brand color extensions and typography theme)
*   **JavaScript (ES6+)** (Dynamic catalog rendering, state management, modal handling, and interactivity)
*   **Font Awesome (v6.4.0)** (Vector icons for sports categories, actions, and UI indicators)
*   **Google Fonts** (Plus Jakarta Sans & Teko typography)
*   **LocalStorage API** (Client-side persistence for product inventory, shopping cart, wishlist, orders, and user sessions)
